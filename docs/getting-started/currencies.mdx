# Balance & Currencies 💰

## Check your balance
`/balance`
This shows you everything you've got — ❄️ flakes, 🧪 vials, and 🍋 lemons. It'll also tell you how much your next card claim costs and roughly how many cards you can grab right now!

## Why currencies matter 🎯

Currencies are the fuel that runs everything! They decide what cards you can claim, what you can draw, and how far you can progress in your collection and guild adventures.

## Flakes ❄️ — Your main spending money

Flakes are **the big one** — you use 'em for everything! Claiming cards, selling cards, auction stuff, buying blueprints and recipes, keeping your guild happy... Flakes make the world go round! 🌍

## Vials 🧪 — For drawing cards

Vials are all about **card draws**. Use 'em to draw new cards, and earn more by liquefying unwanted cards or completing quests. They're a key part of the card progression loop!

## Lemons 🍋 — For special purchases

Lemons are the VIP currency! Use 'em to buy plots, blueprints, and special items in the store. You earn lemons through quests and from buildings you've placed on your guild plots.
