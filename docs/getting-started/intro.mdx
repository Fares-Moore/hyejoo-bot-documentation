## What's the main goal? 🎯

The beauty of Hyejoo is that it's a **sandbox** — you choose your own pace and playstyle:

- 🏆 **Complete card collections** of your favourite idols or groups for clout
- 💎 **Collect rare cards** to flex on your friends
- 🎉 **Join community events** and interact with other players

## How the game usually flows 🌊

Most players start with a simple loop:

1. 💰 **Check `/balance`** — See what resources are available
2. 🌅 **Claim `/daily`** — Get your daily reward
3. 💼 **Use `/work` or `/journey`** — Steady progress while you're around
4. 🃏 **Spend your resources** — On cards, training, or other progression goals

That loop is the foundation of the [Daily To-Do](/docs/daily-to-do/daily-to-do-intro/) section, while the later pages cover the bot's economy, commands, and more advanced systems.

## Key features at a glance 🌟

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 🃏 **Card Collecting** | Claim, trade, forge, and collect cards from hundreds of collections |

| ☕ **Ko-Fi Support** | Premium perks for supporters |

## Quick tips 💡

- 🌅 **Start every session with `/daily`** — It resets everything and gives you fresh currency
- 💰 **Check `/balance` before spending** — Know what you're working with!
- 🃏 **Claim cards in batches** — Use `/claim cards count:5` to save time
- 📋 **Follow `/todo`** — Shows a checklist of things you can do right now
