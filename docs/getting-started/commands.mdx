# Interacting with the bot ⌨️

## Commands

To talk to Hyejoo Bot, just use **slash commands**! Type `/` in your Discord chat and the bot will show you what's available. The basic flow is super simple: check your stuff → claim rewards → grow your collection → level up!

### Handy starter commands 🌱

Here are the ones you'll use the most as a new player:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/balance` 💰 | Check your currencies and see how many cards you can afford |
| `/daily` 🌅 | Claim your daily reward — do this first every time! |
| `/work` 💼 | Earn quick rewards every 10 minutes |
| `/journey` ✈️ | Send your hero on an adventure for a random card |
| `/claim cards` 🃏 | Start building your collection! |
| `/cooldown` ⏱️ | See when your next action is ready |

These are covered in more detail in the [Daily To-Do](../daily-to-do/intro.mdx) section. The later pages dive into the economy and other cool stuff! 🚀
