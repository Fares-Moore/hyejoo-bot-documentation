# Store & Economy 🛒

Hyejoo has a bunch of different stores where you can spend your hard-earned currency! This guide covers how to browse, buy, and use items like a pro. 💰

## Store overview 🏪

The store is super easy to use — just `/store` commands!

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/store view` | Browse store categories and see what's available in each one |
| `/store info` | Peek at the details on a specific item — price, requirements, the works |
| `/store buy` | Grab an item using the right currency! |

### How to shop 🛍️

1. **Start with `/store view`** — You'll see a list of categories like Blueprints, Effect Recipes, and Bonus Items
2. **Pick a category** — Use `/store view store_number:1` (or 2, or 3) to open that shop page
3. **Check out the goods** — Items show their ID, name, price, and a quick description
4. **Get the details** — Use `/store info itemID:item_id` to see full info
5. **Buy buy buy!** — Use `/store buy itemID:item_id` and it's yours!

### Shopping rules 🛒

- **Ticket Store** items are capped at **3 purchases per day**. Resets when you do `/daily`!
- Some items need a minimum player level or an active event to be available.

## Store categories 🗂️

### 🏗️ Store 1 — Blueprints

Blueprints are the plans you need to **build stuff on your guild plots**! Each one costs **lemons 🍋** and needs a certain player level. Once built, they earn you passive lemon income while other players do their thing.

| Blueprint | Price 🍋 | Level Req | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Castle** 🏰 | 150 | 20 | Your first building — gotta have it! Makes 30 🍋/day and boosts storage for other buildings |
| **Gacha Bank** 🏦 | 500 | 30 | Earns 🍋 when guildmates run `/daily` or `/claim` |
| **Tavern** 🍺 | 750 | 40 | Earns 🍋 when someone finishes achievements or quests |
| **Smithing Hub** 🔨 | 1,000 | 50 | Earns 🍋 when people forge, liquefy, or draw cards |
| **Auction House** 🏛️ | 5,000 | 100 | Earns 🍋 when someone wins an auction. Higher levels pay 2x–3x more! |

For full details on building upgrades, check out [Guild Plots & Buildings](./guild-plots) 🏘️

## Inventory commands 🎒

Bought something? It goes straight to your inventory! Here's how to manage your stuff:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/inventory list` | See everything in your bag 🎒 |
| `/inventory info` | Check the details on any item |
| `/inventory use` | Use an item — build a blueprint, craft a recipe, activate a bonus, etc. |
| `/inventory preview` | Peek at what an item does and what it costs before using it |
| `/inventory sell` | Sell something you don't need anymore |

:::tip
Items show up by ID or index number. Use `/inventory list` or `/inventory preview` to see 'em, then `/inventory info index:#` for quick details! 🔍
:::
