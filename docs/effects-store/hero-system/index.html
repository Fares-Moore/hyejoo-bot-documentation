Hero System 🦸

Your hero is your in-game avatar — an idol that you choose to represent you! Heroes aren't just cosmetic though; they're the key to equipping passive effects that boost your gameplay. Let's meet your new sidekick! ✨

What is a hero? 🤔​

A hero is an idol that serves as your personal mascot in Hyejoo. You can pick from a library of preset idols, choose your favourite or whichever one appeals to you the most! Each hero has:

  • A name and ID
  • A level based on XP (earned through guild activity)
  • Followers — how many users have chosen that hero
  • Picture/Gif — prepared by our content team
  • Effect slots — where you equip passive effects for gameplay buffs
tip

Your hero shows up on /hero slots and is how you equip passive effects. Pick someone you love looking at — you'll see them a lot! 💜

Getting your first hero 🎯​

When you first start playing, you won't have a hero yet. Here's how to get one:

1. Browse available heroes​

Use /hero list to see all available heroes. You can filter by name:

CommandWhat it does
/hero listShows all available heroes sorted by level
/hero list hero:nameSearch for a specific hero by name

Heroes are sorted by their level, so popular and active heroes appear first.

2. Set your hero​

Once you've found a hero you like, use /hero get to make them yours:

CommandRequired paramsWhat it does
/hero gethero — Hero name or IDSet a hero as your current hero

What happens when you set a hero:

  • You get a confirmation prompt before switching
  • The hero's follower count goes up (+1)
  • Your previous hero's follower count goes down (-1)
  • A 7-day cooldown starts before you can switch again
warning

You can only change your hero once every 7 days, so choose wisely! If you try to switch too soon, the bot will tell you how long you need to wait. ⏰

3. Check out your hero​

Use /hero show to view your hero and their equipped effect slots:

CommandWhat it does
/hero showView your hero, their level, followers, and equipped effect slots
/hero infoView detailed info about any hero (including who submitted them and their current guild)

Your hero display shows:

  • Hero name and image
  • Level and follower count
  • All your effect slots with their equipped effects (or "Empty" if nothing's equipped)

Effect slots 🎯​

Effect slots are where you equip passive effect cards to get ongoing gameplay buffs. Think of them as gear slots for your hero!

Starting slots​

You begin with 2 passive effect slots by default. When you first try to equip an effect, any missing slots are automatically created for you.

Slot display​

Use /hero slots (or /effect list passives) to see your slots:

AspectDetails
Slot numberWhich slot it is (Slot 1, Slot 2, etc.)
Equipped effectThe name and remaining duration of the equipped passive
CooldownShows when you can swap effects in this slot
DurationIf a slot has a time limit, it's shown next to the slot number

Slot capacity​

Your hero can hold a certain number of passive effects at once:

SourceMax slotsNotes
Default*2★Everyone starts with 2 slots
Effect Slot Upgrade+1 per purchaseBuy from the bonus store for 50,000 🍋
Level limit1 per 100 levelsYou must be level 100, 200, 300, etc. to unlock each upgrade
Upgrade duration210 daysEach upgrade lasts 210 days before the slot expires
tip

At level 100 you can buy your first Effect Slot Upgrade for 50,000 🍋, giving you a 3rd slot for 210 days. At level 200 you can buy a 4th, and so on! 📈

Equipping effects ⚡​

To equip a passive effect to a slot, you need:

  1. A passive effect card (crafted from a recipe — see Effect Cards)
  2. An empty or available slot

/hero equip — How it works​

ParameterRequiredWhat it does
slot✅ YesWhich slot number to equip into (1, 2, 3, etc.)
effect✅ YesThe effect ID or name to equip

Step by step:

  1. Craft a passive effect card from a recipe (buy from store, use from inventory)
  2. Run /hero equip slot:1 effect:tohrugift (or whatever effect you want)
  3. The effect is equipped and starts providing its buff immediately!
  4. If the slot already has an effect, you'll get a confirmation prompt to replace it

Slot cooldown ⏳​

When you equip an effect to a slot, that slot enters a 24-hour cooldown before you can swap effects again. This means you can't freely swap passives in and out — plan your loadout!

ActionCooldown
Equipping an effect to a slot24 hours before you can swap that slot again

What happens when you equip​

  • The passive effect gets an expiry date (if it doesn't already have one from crafting)
  • The effect becomes active and starts providing its buff
  • The slot enters a 24-hour cooldown
tip

Use /hero slots to check which slots are on cooldown before trying to swap effects. The cooldown timer is shown right there! ⏱️

Quick tips 💡​

  • 🎯 Pick a hero you love — You can only change once every 7 days, so make it count!
  • 🛡️ Equip passives early — Effects like Gift From Tohru (better 3★ on first claim) work immediately and are affordable
  • 🔄 Plan your loadout — Each slot has a 24h cooldown when you swap, so think about which passives you want before equipping
  • ⬆️ Save for slot upgrades — At level 100+, buying an Effect Slot Upgrade (50,000 🍋) gives you a huge advantage with more passive slots
