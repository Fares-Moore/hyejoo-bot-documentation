# Effect Cards ✨

Effect Cards are special items that give you ongoing buffs or one-time boosts! Think of 'em like power-ups for your gameplay 🎮 You craft them from the store using **effect recipes**, then equip or use them through your hero.

:::tip
Check out the Quick Player Tips section at the bottom of this page — the player community has some really clever tricks to share!
:::

## How effects work 🛠️

- **Crafting** 🏭 — Buy an effect recipe from `/store buy` (look under Effect Recipes), then use it from your inventory with `/inventory use`. It'll eat some cards from your collection, but hey — you get a cool power-up in return!
- **Passive Effects** 🛡️ — Once crafted, equip passives to your hero's effect slots using `/hero equip`. They hang out there and give you ongoing buffs as long as they're equipped!
    - Your hero starts with **2 passive effect slots** by default. Want more? Grab an **Effect Slot Upgrade** from the bonus store!
- **Active Effects** ⚡ — Use these directly with `/effect use`. Each one has a set number of uses and a little cooldown before you can use it again.
- **Viewing effects** 👀 — Use `/effect list actives` to see your usable effects, or `/effect list passives` (or `/hero slots`) to see what passives you've got equipped.
- **Effect info** 📖 — Use `/effect info` to peep the details on any effect card.

### Passive vs Active — What's the difference? 🤔

| Aspect | 🛡️ Passive Effects | ⚡ Active Effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How they work** | Stay equipped to your hero's slots — they just **do their thing** in the background! | Used one at a time with `/effect use` for a **one-time boost** |
| **How to activate** | `/hero equip` — pop it in a slot and forget about it, it runs automatically | `/effect use` — fire it up when you need it most! |
| **Duration** | Lasts a set number of **days** (e.g., 210 days) then it's goodbye 👋 | Lasts a set number of **uses** (e.g., 20 uses), then it's spent |
| **Cooldown** | **24 hours** before you can swap it out of a slot | Varies per effect (e.g., 15h, 20h, 48h) before you can use it again |
| **Limit per hero** | Up to **2 slots** (more with **Effect Slot Upgrade**) | Unlimited — stack as many as you can craft! |
| **Example** | **Gift From Tohru** — makes your first claim each day extra special 💜 | **Claim Recall** — shaves 4 claims off your claim counter! |

:::note
**Passives** are set-and-forget — equip them once and they work in the background. **Actives** are tactical tools — save them for the right moment! 🎯
:::

| --- | --- |

## Effect slot upgrades

You start with **2 passive effect slots**. To unlock more:

1. Buy an **Effect Slot Upgrade** from **Store 3** (Bonus Items) for 50,000 🍋.
2. You must be at least **level 100** to use it.
3. Each upgrade adds **1 slot** for **210 days**.
4. You can unlock a maximum of **1 slot per 100 levels**.

Use `/hero slots` to see your current slots, their remaining duration, and what effects are equipped.

## Effect Use Increase ⏫

The **Effect Use Increase** is a bonus item from the store that extends the life of your effect cards. It costs **20,000 🍋** from **Store 3 (Bonus Items)** and requires **level 100** to use.
:::tip
You can use `/profile` to check your level! 
:::
### How it works

Depending on whether the targeted effect is passive or active, the item does different things:

| Effect type | What it adds | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Passive Effects** 🛡️ | **+3 days** to the effect's expiration timer | If **Gift From Tohru** has 50 days left, it goes to **53 days** |
| **Active Effects** ⚡ | **+1 use** to the effect's remaining uses | If **Claim Recall** has 5 uses left, it goes to **6 uses** |

**How to use it:**

1. Buy **Effect Use Increase** from the Bonus Store for 20,000 🍋
2. Run `/inventory use item_name:effectincrease effect:effect_id`
3. Replace `effect_id` with the ID of the effect you want to extend (e.g., `tohrugift`, `claimrecall`)

:::tip
Use **Effect Use Increase** on high-value effects like **The Judgment Day** or **Claim Recall** that you use frequently. The 20,000 🍋 price tag is steep, but extra uses on the right effects pay off fast! 📈
:::

## Quick player tips

- **`judgeday` + `spaceunity`** — "judgeday spaceunity" is refered to by players on a regular basis as an efficient source of acquiring 3★ cards for a normal collection, as drawing ★ cards have a relatively steep daily `/draw` star count and vial cost.
    - Players commonly use draw exclusively for completing the 1★ and 2★ portions of a normal collection via `/draw`, 
    - Meanwhile, players are then able to slowly accumulate two 3★ cards at once via **"judgeday spaceunity"** and training them for 4★ card ascends for an entirely different collection, of which they **already own** all of the 1★ and 2★s of.

- **`claimrecall`** is commonly crafted and used by players during boost events, where claiming cards with the designated boost ID have a chance to grant players promo cards.
    - By using `claimrecall` after claiming 5 cards after your claim cost was reset by `/daily`, the total cost of claiming 30 cards to guarantee a promo card goes from:
    <br /> **23,250 flakes** ❄️ (via claiming 30 in one go) 
    <br />--> **17,000 flakes ❄️**, via:
        - **750 ❄️**, (using `claimrecall` after claiming 5 cards) + **16250 ❄️** (claiming an additional 25 cards)
    - This can save players **6250** flakes ❄️ **a day** over the course of the **2 week** boost event, totalling **87,500** flakes ❄️!  
- **`rulerjeanne`** reduces your `/daily` cooldown from 20h to 17h — although uncommon, some players craft this especially for boost events to get them an extra one or two uses of `/daily` over the course of the event.
- **`walpurgisnight`** is a very high priority passive effect for players to craft as it accelerates your normal collection completion rate very rapidly, netting you an extra 2 to 6 cards for your goal collection every day.
- **`cherrybloss`** saves players a very significant amount of flakes ❄️ as forges and ascends are very commonly done by players, espcially when nearing clouts, with ascends giving players duplicate 4★ cards.
