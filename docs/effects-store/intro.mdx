## A quick look at the sub-economy 🏪

Here's how the pieces fit together:

| Piece | What it's about | Currency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🛒 **Store** | Where you spend your hard-earned currency on blueprints, recipes, and bonus items | ❄️ Flakes & 🍋 Lemons |
| 🦸 **Hero** | Your mascot with equip slots for passive effects | Free to pick! |
| ✨ **Effects** | Craftable passive buffs and active boosts | ❄️ Flakes + cards |
| 🏘️ **Guild Plots** | Passive lemon income from buildings | 🍋 Lemons |
