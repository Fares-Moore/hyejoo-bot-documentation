# Effects and Store 💎

:::note
In Hyejoo, the main sub-economy runs on lemons 🍋 — and that's where the store, effects, and plots come in. This section covers everything you can buy, build, craft, and equip to level up your game! 🚀
:::

## What's in this section

The bot's economy goes *way* beyond just claiming cards. Here's what you'll discover:

- 🛒 **[Store & Economy](./store-economy)** — Visit the `/store` to browse categories, buy blueprints, effect recipes, and special items using flakes ❄️, or lemons 🍋
- 🦸 **[Hero System](./hero-system)** — Your in-game avatar! Choose an Anilist character, equip passive effects to slots, and unlock more slots as you level up
- ✨ **[Effect Cards](./effects-list)** — Passive and usable effects that boost your gameplay. Craft them from recipes, equip passives to your hero, and use actives for one-time benefits
- 🏘️ **[Guild Plots & Buildings](./guild-plots)** — Buy plots in your guild, build structures like the Castle, Gacha Bank, Tavern, and more, and earn passive lemon income from other players' actions
- 🏪 **Guild Store** — Server-wide purchases using the guild's balance to build permanent structures that benefit everyone
