# Effects and Store 💎

Beyond just claiming cards, Hyejoo has a whole extra layer of cool stuff to discover! This section is your guide to the **sub-economy** — think of it as the fun side-quests of the game where you can buy, craft, build, and power up your account. 🚀

## What's in this section 🌟

Here's a quick peek at what you'll find:

| Section | What it's about |
| --- | --- |
| 🛒 **[Store & Economy](./store-economy)** | Visit the `/store` to browse what's for sale! Blueprints, effect recipes, special items — spend your flakes ❄️ or lemons 🍋 however you like |
| 🦸 **[Hero System](./hero-system)** | Your in-game mascot! Pick an idol to represent you, equip passive effects to their slots, and unlock more slots as you level up |
| ✨ **[Effect Cards](./effects-list)** | Passive buffs and one-time boosts that make your life easier. Craft 'em from recipes, slap 'em on your hero, and enjoy the perks! |
| 🏘️ **[Guild Plots & Buildings](./guild-plots)** | Buy plots in your guild, build stuff on 'em, and earn **passive lemon income 🍋** while other players go about their day |
| 🏪 **Guild Store** | Server-wide upgrades that help everyone in the guild — paid from the guild's shared balance, not your pocket! |
