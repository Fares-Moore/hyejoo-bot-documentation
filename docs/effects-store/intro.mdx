# Effects and Store 💎

:::note
Tomatoes 🍅 are great for claiming cards, but the *real* economy runs on lemons 🍋 — and that's where the store, effects, and plots come in. This section covers everything you can buy, build, craft, and equip to level up your game! 🚀
:::

## What's in this section

The bot's economy goes *way* beyond just claiming cards. Here's what you'll discover:

- 🛒 **[Store & Economy](./store-economy)** — Visit the `/store` to browse categories, buy blueprints, effect recipes, and special items using tomatoes 🍅, flakes ❄️, or lemons 🍋
- ✨ **[Effect Cards](./effects-list)** — Passive and usable effects that boost your gameplay. Craft them from recipes, equip passives to your hero, and use actives for one-time benefits
- 🏘️ **[Guild Plots & Buildings](./guild-plots)** — Buy plots in your guild, build structures like the Castle, Gacha Bank, Tavern, and more, and earn passive lemon income from other players' actions
- 🏪 **Guild Store** — Server-wide purchases using the guild's balance to build permanent structures that benefit everyone

## How the economy flows 💰

| Currency | Icon | How to earn | Main uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tomatoes** | 🍅 | Daily activities, quests, achievements, work | Claiming cards, buying effect recipes, guild purchases |
| **Flakes** | ❄️ | `/daily` rewards | Store purchases, progressing certain collections |
| **Vials** | 🧪 | Liquefying unwanted cards | `/draw` to get new cards |
| **Lemons** | 🍋 | Guild plots, auctions, events, daily castle income | Blueprints, bonus items, upgrading buildings |

## Start here 🎯

- 🛒 New to the store? Start with the **[Store & Economy](./store-economy)** guide for a full breakdown of categories and what you can buy
- ✨ Want to power up? Jump to the **[Effect Cards](./effects-list)** page for the full list of passive and active effects
- 🏘️ Looking for passive income? Check out **[Guild Plots & Buildings](./guild-plots)** to learn how to build lemon income while you sleep!

## Quick tips 💡

- **Start with a Castle** 🏰 — Before you can build any other plot buildings, you need a Castle first. Save up 150 🍋 and buy a Castle blueprint!
- **Craft effects early** — Passive effects like **Gift From Tohru** (higher 3★ chance on first daily claim) are cheap and give great value
- **Lemons are precious** 🍋 — Save your lemons for blueprints and bonus items. They're harder to earn than tomatoes!
- **Check the guild store** 🏪 — Buildings like the **Lemonade Stand** help everyone in your guild earn more
