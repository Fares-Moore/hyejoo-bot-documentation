How to support 💝​

Ready to support the bot? Here are the simple steps:

  1. 🌐 Head over to our Ko-Fi page and make a donation
  2. 💵 $3 or more = 30 days of premium membership
  3. ✅ Verify your Kofi contribution with our staff
  4. 🎉 Grab your perks!
note

Premium used to activate immediately, but due to some changes with our Ko-Fi integration, donations are now verified manually by staff — hang tight after you donate! ⏳

Premium membership details 📋​

AspectDetails
Minimum donationNone
Premium duration30 days per donation >$3

tip

Private message any staff in Hyejoo Support and they'll direct you on how to get your Ko-Fi verified and your role added!

How to verify your payment ✅​

After donating on Ko-Fi:

  1. 📋 Copy your transaction ID (txid) from your Ko-Fi order page or the order confirmation URL
  2. ⌨️ Copy your Discord ID from your Discord profile
  3. ✅ Message an admin (Renn or Taengu) or a community mod (Aerae) in the Hyejoo Support server, and attach your transaction ID, your Discord username, and a screenshot of your transaction on Ko-Fi!
    • If you've already subscribed to Ko-Fi and still have the role, you can submit the same info in the #kofi-renewal channel instead and our staff will take care of your renewal details
  4. 🕙 Wait for our staff to assign you the role and get access to your benefits

Rewards and benefits 🌟​

Donating to Hyejoo Bot comes with some sweet perks! 🍬 Here's what supporting us gets you:

  • 🎁 Access to the exclusive Ko-Fi channel, where giveaways are hosted on a 2 to 3 week cycle
  • ✏️🎰 Access to the /mark and /koficlaim commands
  • 💫 For $9, you can purchase an already existing card, or a voucher for a future card, from the 4★ signed collection

Donation rewards 🪙​

Just choose the amount you want to donate (special roles at $3, $6+, $9+, $15+ and $21+) and DM Taengu, Renn or a @Community Moderator with a screenshot showing the amount you donated.

These additional rewards are not stackable, and you'll get these rewards everytime you renew:

Ko-Fi subscribers (AmuPlus role) get a daily lottery card claim! Every 24 hours, you can claim a random card from the lottery collection — these special cards aren't available through regular claiming! 🎰

AspectDetails
Cooldown24 hours
RequirementAmuPlus role

Command/koficlaim

Marking your cards with emoji ✏️​

One of the cutest perks of being a Ko-Fi subscriber — you can slap a little emoji marker on your cards! 🌟 Use /mark to add a single emoji to your card, so you can sort, find, and show off your favourites at a glance.

AspectDetails
Command/mark one or /mark many
RequirementKo-Fi subscription (AmuPlus role)
What it doesAdds one emoji marker to your card(s)
Removing marksRun /mark with no emoji to remove the mark 🧹
LimitationOne emoji per card, please! 🙈
note

You'll need to be a Ko-Fi subscriber to use /mark — otherwise the bot will politely tell you to go grab a kofi subscription first! ☕
