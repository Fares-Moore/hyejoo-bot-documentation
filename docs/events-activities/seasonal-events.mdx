# Boost Events 🎄🎃🌸

Boost events are limited-time **promo collections** that pop up during holidays, special seasons and just random collection ideas! 

Think of them as random content drops from the team 🎁 — each event brings exclusive cards you can **only** get during that time. (Excluding reruns) Grab 'em before they're gone!

## How boost events work 🎡

- During a boost event, an **active boostID** will be running in the bot.
- Every **30th card claim** during a boost event guarantees a special promo card🎉 drop when you use the correspending boostID, e.g. `/claim cards boost_ID:sampleboost`
- Boost events usually last between **1 to 2 weeks** — once the event is over, those promos cards are then unclaimable and no new cards from that promo collection can be 'spawned' except for content giveaways.

## Quick tips 💡

- **Stack up during events** — Claim promos to complete your desired collection or to grow your wealth by trading and selling!
- **30-claim guarantee** — Every 30th claim during an active boost = guaranteed special card drop.
- **Use your effects** — Effects such as `claimrecall` and `rulerjeanne` are useful to help you get extra promos and save you some flakes!
