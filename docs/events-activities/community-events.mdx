# Community Events 🌐

Beyond the bot's automated events, the Hyejoo community also organizes its own fun stuff! Server admins and mods run games, giveaways, and other goodies. 🎉

## Types of community events

### Giveaways 🎁

Our content team sometimes runs giveaways where players can win special prizes! These giveaways use Cinnamon and Dyno bot to run:

- **Monthly Flakes GA** — Gives a substantial boost in flakes to the monthly 10 winners. Flake rewards scale based on the winner's existing flake balance.
- **Activity-based rewards** — On occasion, our content team will give out `/daily` resets, `/journey` resets, flake refunds for your `/claim`s, flakes, free recipes, as well as other small bonuses.
- **Card GAs** — The most attractive GAs are of course valuable cards, but these can also include normal card collections, random cards you need for a specified collection, and more!

:::note
Keep an eye on `#giveaways` so you don't miss out on any of these goodies! 👀
:::

### Community Games 🎮

Occasionally, the content team organizes mini-games in the Hyejoo Support Discord server. These games usually include:

- **Rumble Royale** — A Hunger Games-esque bot that determines a random winner with occasional funny interactions 🏹
- **Guessing Games** — Be the first person to correctly guess a randomly selected number or emoji! There are 2 kinds of guessing games hosted by the content team:
  - **Normal** — These give out selected promo cards 🎁
  - **Special** — These contain limited and birthday cards, and come with a 1-month GA winner role. Players holding that role cannot join anything else where the role is excluded.
- **Wheels** — Selected winners will have a random prize selected from a wheel of names! 🎡

### Live Auctions 💎

- Special auction events hosted by the content team where exclusive cards' **concepts** are put up for bid
- These cards have a unique `💎` rarity marker
- Every live auction has its own theme
- The winner submits their idol of choice appropriate to the theme
- The card gets created and added as a custom card (one of a kind) ✨

## How to stay informed 📢

- 📢 **`#announcements`** — Latest bot news and announcements
- 📢 **`#giveaways`** — Where giveaways are announced and hosted
