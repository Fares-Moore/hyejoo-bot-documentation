# Limited-Time Activities ⏰

Besides the regular seasonal events, Hyejoo also runs special one-off activities that add fun new ways to grab cards and rewards! These pop up for a limited time and each one has its own special rules. 🎮

### Group collaborations promos ⭐

These promos are promo collections featuring specific groups — which includes mostly brand and product collaborations of those groups made just for collections' puposes!

Each one has its own unique collection of cards, so keep an eye out for when they run! 👀

:::note
Most of the time, these event cards will drop naturally with `/claim cards`, **without needing a boost_ID**, this also means that there won't be a guaranteed card from these collections during those events on your 30th claims.
:::

## Anniversary Events 🥂

Hyejoo Bot's anniversary celebrations are special events with unique mechanics, exclusive cards, and limited-time recipes!

### How anniversary events work

- A special **anniversary collection** (e.g., `anniversary26` for the 6th anniversary) is created with exclusive cards.
- A limited-time **Hyejoo Anniversary** effect recipe becomes available in the store for **5,000 ❄️**.
- The recipe requires **3× 2★ cards** to craft, and grants **1 use** of the anniversary effect.
- When used, the effect gives you **1 random card** from the anniversary collection.
- You can craft multiple recipes for multiple chances at anniversary cards!

### Anniversary recipe details

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recipe name | **Hyejoo Anniversary** 🥂 |
| Price | 5,000 ❄️ |
| Cards required | 3× 2★ cards |
| Uses per craft | 1 use |
| Cooldown | None |
| Collection | Anniversary collection (e.g., `anniversary26`) |

:::note
Previous anniversaries have also run. For example, `anniversary21` was the 1st anniversary celebration with its own unique cards and rewards!
:::
