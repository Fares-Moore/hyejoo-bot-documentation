## Card types at a glance 🏷️

| Type | Star range | How to get | Rarity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Normal** 🌟 | 1★ – 4★ | `/claim cards`, `/journey` | Common to Uncommon |
| **Legendaries** 👑 | 5★ | `/legroll` 🎟️ | Super rare! |
| **Fragments** 🧩 | 1★ – 4★ | `/claim cards`, `/journey` | Common to Uncommon |
| **Promos** 🎉 | 1★ – 3★ | `/claim cards` during boost events | Event-only |
| **Limited & Birthdays** ⏰ | 4★ | Community events, auctions, giveaways | Very Rare |
| **Live Auction** 💎 | 4★ | Won via live auction events | One of a kind |
| **Specials** ✨ | 4★ | Community events, giveaways, 4★ forges | Rare |

## A good collection loop 🔄

1. 🔍 **Check** your current cards with `/cards` (supports card queries and filters!)
2. ➕ **Add** new ones with `/claim cards` or `/level claim`
3. 🎤 **Show off** your collection with `/summon`
4. 🏷️ **Organize** with `/fav`, `/rate`, `/wish`, or `/lock`
5. ⚔️ **Compare** card ownership with `/diff from` or `/diff for`
6. 📊 **Evaluate** with `/eval` before selling or liquefying

## Quick reference — collection commands at a glance 🎮

| Command | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| `/cards` | Browse your collection with optional card queries and the `global` flag for the full pool |
| `/summon` | Display a single card in all its glory |
| `/wish` | Track cards you want to acquire later |
| `/info` | Check a card's details |
| `/fav` / `/rate` | Personalize your collection and keep your faves easy to find |
| `/lock` | Protect valuable cards from accidental actions |
| `/diff from` / `/diff for` | Compare your collection with another player |
