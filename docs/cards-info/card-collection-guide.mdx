# Card Collection Guide 🖼️

:::tip
Think of your card collection as a little gallery of your favorite idols and moments. Some players chase shiny stars, while others focus on completing specific sets or collecting special event cards.
:::

## Normal Cards (1★–4★) ⭐

These are the everyday cards that make up the bulk of most collections. They form the backbone of your collection progress from your claims.

| Star level | Rarity | Claim rate | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1★ | Common | ~47% | Fodder for combines, liquefying |
| 2★ | Common | ~47% | Fodder for combines, liquefying, effect recipes |
| 3★ | Uncommon | ~5% | Core collection pieces, ascension material |
| 4★ | Rare | ~1% | Valuable collection pieces, high-value ascension targets |

### How to get them

- `/claim cards` — Main source of normal cards
- `/journey` — Random card chance every 12 hours
- `/draw` — Spend vials for a chance at cards up to 3★ (or 6★ with **Walpurgis Night** effect)
- `/forge` — Combine two same-rarity cards into a new card
- `/ascend` — Combine two maxed cards into a higher-star card

### What to do with duplicates

| Action | Result | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Combine** ➡️ | Fodder card gives XP to target card | Leveling up clout targets |
| **Forge** 🔨 | Two same-rarity cards → new card + vials | Recycling unwanted cards |
| **Ascend** ⬆️ | Two maxed cards → higher star card | Pushing to 4★ cards |
| **Liquefy** 🧪 | Cards → vials for `/draw` | Building vial reserves |
| **Sell** 💰 | Cards → Flakes ❄️ | Quick flakes |

## Legendaries (5★) 👑

Legendary cards are the big dream pulls — the rarest cards in the game at 5★ rarity. They often become the centerpiece of a player's collection.

### How to get legendaries

| Method | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **`/legroll`** 🎲 | Spend a **Legendary Card Claim Ticket** (from collection resets) for a guaranteed 5★ |
| **Events** 🎉 | Occasionally given out during special community events and giveaways |

### Legendary cards in gameplay

- Basically just a trophy for completing a collection (lol) 🏆
- Cannot be used in `/forge`, `/ascend`, or `/combine`
- Use `/info` to check a legendary card's total owners and demand
- Protect your legendaries with `/lock` and `/fav`!
- The **Legendary Card Swapper** (10,000,000 ❄️ in the bonus store, level 100+) lets you swap a legendary for another random one in the same collection

:::tip
Save your legendary tickets! Since the collection is determined when the ticket is awarded (not when you roll), you can hold onto tickets for when new legendaries get added to the pool. 🎯
:::

## Fragments 🧩

Fragment collections work differently from normal collections. They use a unique fragment system where you collect base fragments and upgrade them.

### How fragments work

- Fragment collections focus on collecting the **1★ base cards**
- Higher star cards in fragment collections come from merging/upgrading fragments
- When checking collection completion (`/collection list`), only 1★ cards are counted for fragment collections
- Fragment cards cannot be used in forging
- Fragment collections are typically based on **subunits or special project groups** in K-pop (e.g., LOONA OEC, IZ*ONE Someday, WJSN Chocome)

### How to get fragment cards

| Star level | Source |
| --- | --- |
| 1★ (base) | `/claim cards`, `/journey` |
| 4★ (merged) | Completing a fragment collection reset |

## Specials ✨

Special collections are tied to unique events, mechanics, or exclusive releases. These are less common and more desirable than normal cards.

### How to get special cards

| Method | What it gives |
| --- | --- |
| **4★ Forges** 🏭 | Random special card from the available pool |
| **Community events** 🎪 | Given out during events and giveaways |
| **Giveaways** 🎁 | Distributed through server activities |

### Special collection properties

Some collections have special tags that affect how their cards behave:

| Tag | Behavior | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `special` | Solo debuts or releases by idols — obtainable via 4★ forges or giveaways | Loona solo debuts |
| `lottery` | Involved events, collaborations, or brand endorsements — obtained via lottery draws or forges | Collaboration cards |
| `album` | Cards representing specific album releases (boy/girl albums) — forge rules apply | `bgalbums`, `ggalbums` |

## Promos 🎉

Promo cards are tied to promotional or limited-release content. They're especially valuable for showing off rare parts of your collection.

### How promos work

- Acquired during **boost events** with the corresponding boost ID
- Use `/claim cards boost_id:eventname` during an active boost event
- **Every 30 claims guarantees** an event promo card drop during the boost period
- Promo collections have their own forging rules

### Promo collection examples

| Boost ID | Theme | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `halloween25` | 🎃 Halloween | 2025 |
| `christmas25` | 🎄 Christmas | 2025 |
| `spring25` | 🌷 Spring | 2025 |
| `ivesserafim` | 💋 Special collab | 2025 |
| `chuseok24` | 🌕 Chuseok | 2024 |

### Promo forging restrictions

| Cards forged | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Two promos from the same promo collection** ✅ | A promo card from that collection |
| **Two promos from different promo collections** ❌ | A **normal** card — not a promo! |
| **Promo + normal card** ❌ | A normal card |

:::warning
Forging promos from different collections wastes the promo value — the result won't be a promo card!
:::

## Limited & Birthday cards ⏰

These cards celebrate special moments, seasonal themes, or celebratory events. They are very exclusive with only **3 maximum owners per card**.

### Characteristics

| Type | How to get | Max owners |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Limited** 🔥 | Community events, collabs, brand endorsements | 3 |
| **Birthdays** 🍰 | Birthday celebration events | 3 |
| **Anniversary** 🥂 | Bot anniversary events (e.g., Hyejoo Anniversary) | Event-based |

### Collecting tips

- Check `/boost list` to see if any events are running
- Limited and Birthday cards often have **higher trade value** than normal cards
- **Don't liquefy or sell limited cards** — they may be gone forever!
- Cannot be used in forging (excluded collection type)

## Live Auction cards 💎

Live auction cards are the **rarest of the rare** — one-of-a-kind cards created through community events.

### How live auctions work

Liveauctions are special events where the community participates in a **live bid** to **create** a brand new custom card according to the event's theme. The idol and the image chosen will be provided by the winning bidder. The resulting card is absolutely **one of a kind**.

### Live Auction forging restrictions

- Cards from `liveauction` flagged collections **cannot** be used in `/forge`
- They can normally be sold, traded, or kept as collection pieces

## Signed cards 💫

Signed/autograph variant cards are **exceedingly rare** — only given out during specific giveaway events.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| How to get | Exclusive giveaway events |
| Rarity | Exceedingly Rare |
| Forgeable? | No |

## Misc cards 🎲

This category covers cards that don't fit neatly into the main groups — seasonal exclusives, giveaway rewards, and other special drops.

### Examples

| Type | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Event rewards** 🏅 | Community activities | Given for participation or winning |
| **Giveaway cards** 🎁 | Promotional giveaways | Various rarity levels |
| **Milestone cards** 🏆 | Reaching certain goals | Bot milestones, collection goals |
| **Legacy cards** 📜 | Past systems or discontinued mechanics | Cannot be obtained anymore |
| **Staff Exclusive** 🏵️ | Content team creations | Staff-exclusive collections |
| **Fanarts** 🎨 | Fan art submissions | Community-created art cards |

## Quick tips for your collection journey

- **1★ and 2★ cards** are great for liquefying into vials (especially with **The Holy Grail** effect active).
- **3★ and 4★ cards** are your main collection pieces — keep them for collection completion.
- **Legendaries** are irreplaceable — lock and favorite them immediately.
- **Promo and Limited cards** are time-sensitive — grab them while you can!
- Check `/collection list` regularly to track your progress.
- Use `/info` on a card to see its **price, global owners, demand (wishlist count), and average rating** before making big decisions!
