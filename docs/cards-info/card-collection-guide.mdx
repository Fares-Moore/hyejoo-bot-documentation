# Card Collection Guide 🖼️

:::tip
Think of your card collection as a little gallery of your favorite idols! Some players chase shiny stars ⭐, others focus on completing sets or collecting event cards. There's no wrong way to enjoy it!
:::

## Normal cards (1★–4★) ⭐

These are the everyday cards that make up most collections — your bread and butter!

| Star level | Rarity | How common? | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1★ | Common | ~47% of claims | Combine fodder, liquefying |
| 2★ | Common | ~47% of claims | Combine fodder, liquefying, effect recipes |
| 3★ | Uncommon | ~5% of claims | Core collection pieces, ascension |
| 4★ | Rare | ~1% of claims | Valuable collection pieces, high-value ascension |

### How to get them

- `/claim cards` — Main source of normal cards
- `/journey` — Random card chance every 12 hours
- `/draw` — Spend vials for a chance at cards up to 3★ (or 6★ with **Walpurgis Night** effect)

### What to do with duplicates

- **Combine/Forge** — Turn two cards of the same star level into a new card
- **Liquefy** — Convert into vials 🧪 for `/draw`
- **Sell** — Sell for Flakes ❄️
- **Effect recipes** — Many recipes require sacrificing specific card rarities

## Legendaries (5★) 🌟

Legendary cards are the big dream pulls — the rarest cards in the game. They often become the centerpiece of a player's collection.

### How to get legendaries

| Method | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **`/legroll`** | Spend a **Legendary Card Claim Ticket** for a guaranteed 5★ |

### Legendary cards in gameplay

- Basically just a trophy for completing a collection (lol)
- Cannot be used in `/forge` or `/combine`
- Cannot be ascended
- Use `/info` to check a legendary card's total owners
- Protect your legendaries with `/lock` and `/fav`!
- The **Legendary Card Swapper** (10,000,000 ❄️ in the bonus store) lets you swap a legendary for another random one in the same collection

## Fragments 🧩

Fragment collections work differently from normal collections. They use a fragment system where you collect base fragments and upgrade them.

### How fragments work

- Fragment collections focus on collecting the **1★ base cards**
- Higher star cards in fragment collections come from merging/upgrading fragments
- When checking collection completion (`/collection list`), only 1★ cards are counted for fragment collections
- Fragment cards cannot be used in forging

## Nonstandard cards ✨

Nonstandard cards are tied to events, mechanics, or unique collection goals. These are less common and more desirable than normal cards.

### Where to find Nonstandard cards

- **Special collections** — Collections marked with special properties (rarity, lottery, etc.)
- **Event rewards** — Given out during community events, boost events and giveaways

### Special collection sources

Nonstandard collections originate from different ideas by our content team!

| Property | Source | Star Count | Rarity | 
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lottery` | Involved events, collaborations, or brand endorsements for groups/soloists | 1★ - 4★ | Uncommon |
| `album` | Cards representing album releases | 4★ | Rare |
| `fragments` | Based on group subunits | 1★ or 4★ | Common |
| `liveauction` | Community created cards, adheres to the the liveauction's theme and the idol selected | 4★ | 1 of a Kind |
| `birthdays` | Birthday celebration cards | 4★ | Extremely Rare (3 maximum owners) | 
| `limited` | Collaborations, or brand endorsements for groups/soloists | 4★ | Extremely Rare (3 maximum owners) | 
| `signed` | Signed/autograph variant cards | 4★ | Exceedingly Rare (Only given out during specific giveaway events) |
| `special` | Solo debuts or releases by idols | 4★ | Rare, from 4★ forges or giveaways |

## Promo cards 🎉

Promo cards are tied to promotional or limited-release content. They're especially valuable for showing off rare parts of your collection.

### How promos work

- Acquired during **boost events** with the according boost ID 
- Use `/claim cards boostID:` during an active boost event
- Every 30 claims will grant a guarenteed event promo during the boost event 
- Promo collections have their own forging rules — two promo cards from the same collection can forge into another promo from the same collection

### Promo restrictions

- **Two promo cards from the same collection can't be forged into another promo, instead grants a random normal card.**
- Promo cards forged with a normal card will grant a random normal card.

## Limited & Birthday cards ⏰

These cards celebrate special moments, seasonal themes, or celebratory events. They are very exclusive and often highly sought after.

### Characteristics

- **Limited** — Available only from giveaways and community-hosted events.
- **Birthdays** — Available only from giveaways and community-hosted events.
- **Anniversary** — Released for bot anniversary celebrations (e.g., Hyejoo Anniversary26 collection)
- Cannot be used in forging (excluded collection type)

### Collecting tips

- Check `/boost list` to see if any boost events are running
- Limited and Birthdays cards often have higher trade value
- Don't liquefy or sell limited cards — they may be gone forever!

## Live Auction cards 🏛️

Live auction are annual events where the community does a livebid to **create** a brand new custom card according to the theme. The idol and the image chosen will be provided by the winning bidder. Resulting card will be absolutely one of a kind.

### Live Auction restrictions in forging

- Cards from `liveauction` flagged collections cannot be used in `/forge`
- They can normally be sold, traded, or kept as collection pieces

## Miscellaneous cards 🎲

This category covers cards that don't fit neatly into the main groups — seasonal cards, giveaway rewards, and other special drops.

### Examples

- **Event rewards** — Given out during community activities
- **Giveaway cards** — Distributed through promotional giveaways
- **Milestone cards** — Rewarded for reaching certain goals
- **Legacy cards** — From past systems or discontinued mechanics

## How to grow your collection

A good collection routine usually looks like:

1. **Claim daily** — Start with `/daily`, then `/claim cards` for new pulls
2. **Browse and organize** — Use `/cards` to see what you have, `/wish` to track targets
3. **Evaluate** — Use `/eval` to know what's worth keeping vs selling
4. **Compare** — Use `/diff from` or `/diff for` to benchmark against other players
5. **Progress** — Train, ascend, and work toward collection completion

## Quick tips

- **1★ and 2★ cards** are great for liquefying into vials (especially with **The Holy Grail** effect active).
- **3★ and 4★ cards** are your main collection pieces — keep them for collection completion.
- **Legendaries** are irreplaceable — lock and favorite them immediately.
- **Promo and Limited cards** are time-sensitive — grab them while you can!
- Check `/collection list` regularly to track your progress.
