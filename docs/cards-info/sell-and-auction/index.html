Sell & Auction House 🏪💰

Got extra cards? There are two ways to turn 'em into Flakes ❄️ — sell 'em directly to the bot, or list 'em on the Auction House for other players to bid on! Let's get into it! 🏪

Selling to the bot 🤖​

warning

If you don't specify a user during /sell, it will default to a bot sell, please exercise caution so you don't lose valuable cards!

The easiest way to get rid of cards you don't need! Sell 'em straight to the bot for a quick payout in Flakes ❄️.

CommandRequired paramsWhat it does
/sell oneCard query, optional target userSell a single card to the bot (or another player)
/sell manyCard query, optional count, optional target userSell multiple cards at once
/sell previewCard queryPreview the total value before selling

How selling works​

  • The bot pays you 40% of the card's evaluation when you sell to it.
  • Selling to another player uses 100% of the card's evaluation as the price.
  • Locked cards are excluded from selling by default — add locked:true to include them.
  • Favorited cards with only 1 copy are protected from being sold.

Selling to other players 👥​

You can sell cards directly to another player — no auction house needed! Just add their user ID:

/sell one card_query user_id:123456789

AspectDetails
Price100% of the card's evaluation
PaymentBuyer pays in Flakes ❄️
NotificationThe other player gets a prompt to accept or decline
PendingIf they don't respond immediately, a pending transaction is created
Missing filterUse missing:true to sell only cards the other player doesn't have
tip

Selling directly to another player bypasses auction fees — perfect for guildmates and friends! 🤝

Transactions 📋​

When you sell to another player, a transaction is created. Both parties can manage it:

CommandWhat it does
/transaction confirmAccept a pending transaction and complete the exchange
/transaction declineReject a pending transaction
/transaction listView all your pending and completed transactions
/transaction infoCheck details on a specific transaction

How transactions work​

  1. Seller runs /sell one card user_id:player123
  2. The buyer gets a confirmation prompt
  3. If the buyer accepts — flakes are transferred and the card moves to their inventory ✨
  4. If the buyer doesn't accept immediately and the button prompts dissapear — a pending transaction is created
  5. Both parties can use /transaction list to find it and /transaction confirm to finish it later

The Auction House 🏛️​

Want to get the best price for a rare card? List it on the Auction House and let players bid it up! This is where the big money moves happen. 💰

CommandRequired paramsWhat it does
/auction listOptional filters (card query, me, diff, bid)Browse active auctions
/auction infoAuction IDView full details on a specific auction
/auction previewOptional filtersPreview active auctions one at a time
/auction sellCard query, optional price & timeList a card for auction
/auction bidAuction ID + bid amountPlace a bid on an auction
/auction cancelAuction IDCancel your auction (before anyone bids)

Listing a card for auction 🏷️​

Ready to sell? Here's what you need to know:

AspectDetails
Price rangeBetween 50% and 400% of the card's evaluated value
Duration1 to 10 hours (default is 6 hours)
Listing fee% of your set price (percentage varies by server, reduced by Discount Center building)
What you CAN'T auctionLegendary (5★) cards, lottery cards, jackpot cards, and signed cards
Protected cardsLocked cards can't be sold; favorited single-copy cards get a warning
tip

Set your price strategically! Too high and nobody bites, too low and you might undersell. Check /auction list to see what similar cards are going for! 📈

Listing fee calculation​

The listing fee is a percentage of your set price. But if your guild has a Discount Center building, the fee gets cheaper!

Fee = Price × (auctionFeePercent / 100) × Discount_Center_multiplier

Discount Center levelFee reduction
No building0% (full fee)
Level 1-5%
Level 2-10%
Level 3-15%
Level 4-20%

Bidding on auctions 🏏​

Found a card you want? Here's how to win it:

AspectDetails
Minimum bidMust be higher than the current price
Bid attemptsMax 5 bids per auction per person
Outbid refundIf you're outbid, your old bid amount is refunded
Sniping protectionIf a bid comes in the last 5 minutes, the auction extends by 3 minutes
Increasing your bidYou can increase your own bid up to 5 times
Can't bid on your ownSelf-bidding is not allowed

Winning an auction 🎉​

When the timer runs out and you're the highest bidder:

  1. The card is added to your inventory 🎴
  2. You pay your winning bid amount
  3. If you have the Skies of Friendship ☁️ passive effect, you get 13% cashback!

Cashback = Winning_Bid × 0.13

Cancelling an auction ⛔​

You can cancel your auction, but only if:

  • Nobody has bid on it yet
  • There's more than 1 hour left on the timer

Cancelling does not refund the listing fee.

Auction notifications​

The bot will DM you about auction activity if you have notifications enabled:

NotificationWhen it triggers
New bid on your auctionSomeone bids on your listing
You've been outbidSomeone outbids you on an auction
Wishlist alertA card on your wishlist is listed — this works for everyone, even without an effect! 🎯
tip

The Festival of Wishes 🎐 passive used to be needed for wishlist auction notifications, but now it's a free feature for everyone! You can skip crafting that one. 😄

Sell vs Auction — which to choose? 🤔​

ScenarioBest choice
Quick flakes, don't mind the discount/sell one to bot 🤖
Trading with a friend or another server member/sell one user_id: 👥
Rare card that others might wantAuction House 🏛️
You're in a hurry (1 hour listings)Auction with timeLength:1 ⏰
Multiple cards to unload/sell many 📦

Quick tips 💡​

  • Check /auction list before listing to see what's already on market
  • Use /sell preview to check values before committing
  • High demand cards (popular idols, rare collections) do best on auction
  • Use the miss filter — /auction list card_query:-miss shows cards you don't own yet
  • Watch your bids — you only get 5 bid attempts per auction, so bid smart!
  • Discount Center matters — if your guild has one, auction fees are way cheaper 🏗️
