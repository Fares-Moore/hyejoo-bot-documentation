# Work 💼 — Quick Rewards!

## `/work`

Clock in and earn some resources every **10 minutes**! Sometimes things go wrong (hey, not every shift is perfect 😅), but most of the time you'll walk away with a nice little payday.

### What you can earn 💰

| Reward | How much | Chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flakes ❄️ | A small amount | Common 🟢 |
| Vials 🧪 | A small amount | Common 🟢 |
| User XP ⬆️ | Some exp | Always 🟢🟢 |
| Nothing 😅 | — | Uncommon 🟡 *(sometimes work doesn't pay off!)* |

### Command parameters

| Parameter | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(none)* | — | Just `/work` and earn! No extra parameters needed. |

### When to use it ⏰

- **During cooldown downtime** — While waiting for `/daily` or `/journey`, `/work` is a perfect filler
- **When you're just checking in** — Even if you're not actively playing, a quick `/work` keeps your balance moving
- **As a routine habit** — Pop in every 10 minutes for small gains here and there

:::tip
Use `/work` when you want a **low-effort way to earn** while you're hanging out in Discord or doing other things. It's one of the easiest commands to fit into your routine! 🎯
:::

### Related commands

| Command | Why it's useful |
| --- | --- |
| `/cooldown` | Check when work is ready again (10 min cooldown) |
| `/daily` | Start your earning loop for the day |
| `/journey` | Send your hero on an adventure for a card |
