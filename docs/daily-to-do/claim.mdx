# Claiming Cards 🃏

## `/claim cards`

This is *the* main way to build your collection! You spend **Flakes ❄️** (your main claim currency) to pull random cards from the pool. During special events, you can also use **boost IDs** to snag limited-edition cards.

### How claim cost works 📈

Every card you claim makes the **next claim a little pricier**. Your claim cost resets when you use `/daily`, so plan your sessions!

| Claims today | Cost per claim |
| --- | --- |
| First claim | Starts at **50** ❄️ |
| Each additional claim | Goes up by **50** ❄️ each time (We recommend claiming between 6-10 cards a day!) |
| Every 30 claims | Guarantees a **promo card drop** during active boost events! 🎉 |

### Command parameters

| Parameter | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `count` | ❌ Optional | Number of cards to claim in one go (max **10** per command) |
| `boost` | ❌ Optional | Use a specific **boost ID** during events for special promo drops |
| `group_type` | ❌ Optional | Filter by group type (e.g., `girlgroups`, `boygroups`) |
| `promo` | ❌ Optional ***(deprecated)*** | Spend promo currency during special events |
| `any` | ❌ Optional ***(deprecated)*** | Bypass guild lock restrictions |

### Tips for beginners 🌱

- **Check your balance first!** — Use `/balance` to see how many ❄️ you have before diving in.
- **Claim in batches** — Smaller batches keep costs manageable. Use `/claim cards count:5` to start.
- **Start your day with `/daily`** — It resets your claim cost so you get the best prices.
- **Watch for events** — During active events, your **30th claim** guarantees a bonus promo card drop!

### Special claim bonuses ✨

| Bonus | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Boost drops** 🎁 | During active boosts, every **30th claim** guarantees a special promo card drop. Check `/todo` to see if any boosts are active! |
| **Gift From Tohru** 🐉 | If you have this passive equipped and haven't claimed any cards today, your **first claim** has a higher chance at a **3★** card! |
| **Cake Day**  | If you have this passive equipped,gGet +100 more flakes on `/daily` for every claim you did in the previous day! |

## `/level claim`

An alternate way to claim cards that uses **level-based logic** instead of the standard claim mechanics. It can give different card results compared to `/claim cards`!

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Parameters | Same optional params as `/claim cards` — `count`, `promo`, `any`, `boost`, `group_type` |
| Best for | Players who want a different **card level distribution** than regular claiming |
| Cost | Same scaling as `/claim cards` — costs increase with each claim |

:::tip
`/level claim` will give you cards that have a chance to have levels for you to combine! Learn more about combines [here](https://fares-moore.github.io/hyejoo-bot-documentation/docs/clouting/training-ascension#combine--fuse-fodder-cards). 🎲
:::
