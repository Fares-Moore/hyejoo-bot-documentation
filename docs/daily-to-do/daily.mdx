# Daily 🌅 — Start Here!

## `/daily`

This is **THE most important command** in the whole game! Claim your daily flakes every **20 hours** (or **17 hours** if you've got the **Ruler Jeanne** passive effect!). Think of it as your morning coffee ☕ for card collecting — it wakes everything up!

Running `/daily` resets your claim costs, refreshes your quests, and gives you a nice overview of what's happening in the bot. Don't skip it!

### What you get 💰

| Reward | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Base flakes | **500** ❄️ |
| Streak bonus | **+5** ❄️ for each consecutive day you keep the streak going |
| Daily summary | A handy overview of active events, boosts, announcements, and any pending trades |

### Command parameters

| Parameter | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(none)* | — | Just run `/daily` and you're all set! No extra parameters needed. |

### Why it's so important 💡

- **Resets your claim cost** — The more you claim in a day, the more expensive it gets. `/daily` brings it back to the starting price.
- **Refreshes daily quests** — You get a new set of quests every day when you claim your daily.
- **Restocks store limits** — Store purchase limits (like the Ticket Store's 3-per-day cap) reset with your daily.
- **Streak rewards add up** — Every day you keep your streak is another +5 ❄️. Miss a day and it resets!

:::tip
Make `/daily` your very first stop every time you hop into the bot! It refreshes everything and sets the stage for your whole session. 💪
:::
