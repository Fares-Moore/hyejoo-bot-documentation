## A simple beginner routine 🌱

Here's a solid daily loop to get you started:

1. 🌅 **`/daily`** — Claim your daily flakes and refresh everything
2. 💰 **`/balance`** — Check how many flakes you've got
3. 🃏 **`/claim cards`** — Start building your collection!
4. 💼 **`/work`** — Earn quick rewards between cooldowns
5. ✈️ **`/journey`** — Send your hero on an adventure for a free card
6. ⏱️ **`/cooldown`** — See what's ready next and plan ahead

## Quick reference — all cooldowns at a glance ⏰

| Command | Cooldown | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/daily` | 20h (17h with Ruler Jeanne) | 500+ ❄️ flakes, resets claim cost |
| `/work` | 10 min | ❄️ Flakes, 🧪 Vials, or XP |
| `/train` | 15 min | Card XP toward leveling up |
| `/journey` | 12h | Random card (2★–4★, occasional promos) |
| `/vote` | 12h | Voting rewards |
| `/koficlaim` | 24h | Lottery card (AmuPlus only) |
| `/kofipromo` | 30 days | Monthly promo card (subscribers only) |
