# Journey ✈️ — Free Cards!

## `/journey`

Pack your bags and head out on a little adventure! 🎒 Every **12 hours**, you can send your hero on a journey and come back with a **random card**. It's a chill, low-effort way to grow your collection while waiting on other cooldowns.

### What you can get 🎁

| Card type | Chance | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2★** cards | Common 🟢 | Most journeys will bring back a 2★ |
| **3★** cards | Uncommon 🟡 | A nice little bonus when luck is on your side! |
| **4★** cards | Rare 🟠 | Rare but possible — a great surprise! |
| **Event promo cards** | Extremely Rare 🟣 |  Random promo card drop if you're extremely lucky! |
| **Album cards** | Very Rare 🎵 | A chance at album-exclusive cards too! |

### Command parameters

| Parameter | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(none)* | — | Just `/journey` and go! No extra parameters needed. |

### When to use it ⏰

- **After your daily routine** — Claim your daily, do some claims, then send your hero on a journey
- **Every 12 Hours** — A journey is perfect for trying to fill your collection with rare cards
- **As part of your log-in loop** — `/daily` → `/work` → `/journey` is a solid routine!

:::tip
Think of journeys as a **bonus layer of progression** rather than your main source of cards. They're a fun little treat every 12 hours! 🎉
:::

### Related commands

| Command | Why it's useful |
| --- | --- |
| `/cooldown` | Check when your next journey is ready |
| `/work` | Earn quick rewards while you wait |
| `/daily` | Start your day right before sending your hero off |
