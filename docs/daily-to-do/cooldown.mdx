# Cooldown ⏱️ — Never Guess Again!

## `/cooldown`

Ever found yourself wondering "wait, when's my daily ready?" or "can I work yet?" — `/cooldown` has your back! This handy command shows you **all your active cooldowns at a glance**, so you can plan your next moves without the guesswork. No more awkwardly clicking commands just to be told "nope, not yet!" 😅

### What it tracks 📋

| Action | Cooldown | Shown? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/daily` 🌅 | 20 hours (17h with Ruler Jeanne) | ✅ |
| `/work` 💼 | 10 minutes | ✅ |
| `/train` 💪 | 15 minutes | ✅ |
| `/journey` ✈️ | 12 hours | ✅ |
| `/koficlaim` ☕ | 24 hours | ✅ |

### Command parameters

| Parameter | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(none)* | — | Just run it and see everything! |

### Why it's super helpful 🙌

- **Plan your whole session** — See what's ready now and what to wait for
- **No more wasted clicks** — Stop trying commands that are still on cooldown
- **One place for everything** — No need to remember when you last worked or trained
- **Great for quick check-ins** — Pop in, do what's ready, and go about your day!

:::tip
Make `/cooldown` your **second stop** after `/daily` — it'll plan out your whole session! 📋
:::
