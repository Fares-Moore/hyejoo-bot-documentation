# Cooldown ⏱️

## `/cooldown`

Ever wondered "when's my daily ready again?" or "can I work yet?" — `/cooldown` has the answers! This handy command shows you **all your active cooldowns at a glance**, so you can plan your next moves without the guesswork.

### What it tracks 📋

| Action | Cooldown | Shown in `/cooldown`? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/daily` 🌅 | 20 hours (17h with Ruler Jeanne) | ✅ |
| `/work` 💼 | 10 minutes | ✅ |
| `/train` 💪 | 15 minutes | ✅ |
| `/journey` ✈️ | 12 hours | ✅ |
| `/vote` 🗳️ ***(Currently not working)*** | 12 hours | ❌ |
| `/koficlaim` ☕ | 24 hours | ✅ |
| `/*********` ☕ |  ??? (Coming Soon!) | ❔ |

### Command parameters

| Parameter | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(none)* | — | Just `/cooldown` and see everything at once! |

### Why it's super helpful 🙌

- **Plan your routine** — See what's ready now and what you should wait for
- **Avoid wasted clicks** — No more trying commands only to be told they're on cooldown
- **Track everything in one place** — No need to remember when you last worked or trained
- **Perfect for multi-tasking** — Pop in, check cooldowns, do what's ready, and come back later

:::tip
Make `/cooldown` your **second stop** after `/daily` to plan out your whole session! 📋
:::
