# Train 💪

## `/train`

Help your cards grow stronger by training them! Each training session gives **experience points (XP)** to a card, helping it level up. This becomes super important once you start aiming for **ascension** (combining two max-level cards into a higher-star card) or just want to level up your favorites.

### Command parameters

| Parameter | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `card_query` | ✅ Yes | Which card to train (card name or query — see [query library](#)) |
| *(none)* | — | Just pick a card and train it! |

### Training details 📊

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cooldown | **15 minutes** between training sessions |
| What you get | **Card XP** toward the next level |
| Best for | Cards you plan to **ascend** or want to max out |
| Level cap | Once a card is max level, you can **ascend** it with another maxed card! (Level 50 for 2* cards, Level 100 for 3* Cards) |

### When training matters most 🔑

- **Preparing for ascension** — Two max-level cards of the same star rating can be ascended into a higher-star card
- **Building your best cards** — Level up your favorite pulls and flex them on your profile
- **Collection goals** — Some collections or achievements may benefit from leveled-up cards

:::tip
Pair training with `/cards` to pick your best candidates and `/collection reset` to see how your collection is growing! 📈
:::

### Training strategy 🎯

1. **Pick your targets** — Use `/cards` to find cards you want to level up
2. **Train consistently** — Every 15 minutes, train the card until it reaches max level
3. **Combine and ascend** — Once you have two maxed cards of the same ★ rating, use `/ascend` to combine them
4. **Repeat!** — Ascending cards is needed to help you progress further to your clouts!

### Related commands

| Command | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| `/cards` | Find cards to train in your collection |
| `/ascend` | Combine two maxed cards into a higher-star card |
| `/profile` | Show off your collection and card progress |
| `/cooldown` | Check when training is ready again |
