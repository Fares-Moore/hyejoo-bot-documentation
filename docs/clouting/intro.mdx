# Clouts and Progression 🏆

Clouts are how you show off your collection! In Hyejoo, "clouting" means completing card collections, ranking up your cards, and progressing through the game's many systems. This section covers everything related to growing your account and flexing your achievements. Ready to become a card-collecting legend? Let's go! 🌟

## What is clouting? 🤔

Clouting is the main endgame loop. When you complete a card collection (get every card in a set), you earn the right to **clout** it — marking it as completed and showing off your achievement. The more collections you complete, the more clout you earn. It's the ultimate flex! 💪

## The clouting loop 🔄

1. 🗓️ **Start with `/daily`** — Reset your claim cost and get fresh quests
2. 🃏 **Claim cards** — Use `/claim cards` or `/level claim` to grow your collection
3. 💪 **Train your best cards** — Build up XP with `/train`
4. ⬆️ **Ascend and forge** — Combine maxed cards for higher rarities
5. 👑 **Go for legendaries** — Save up tickets for `/legroll` to get that 5★ flex
6. 📊 **Track your collections** — Use `/collection list` to see what's missing

## Progression commands by system

### Daily commands 🗓️

| Command | Cooldown | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/daily` | 20h (17h with Ruler Jeanne) | ❄️ Flakes + resets claim cost |
| `/claim cards` | Cost-based | Random cards from unlocked collections |
| `/level claim` | Cost-based | Level-boosted cards (up to 40% of max level) |

### Card improvement commands 💪

| Command | Cooldown | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/train` | 15 min | Gains XP toward next card level |
| `/ascend` | None | Two maxed same-rarity cards → next rarity |
| `/combine` | None | Fodder card → XP for target card |
| `/forge` | None | Two same-rarity cards → new card + vials 🧪 |
| `/liquefy` | None | Cards → vials 🧪 |
| `/draw` | None | Vials 🧪 → random card (up to 3★ daily, 6★ with Walpurgis) |

### Collection commands 📊

| Command | Optional params | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/collection list` | `cols`, `completed`, `clouted`, `sortComplete` | Show filtered collection progress |
| `/collection info` | `collection` (name/ID) | View cards in a collection and your ownership |
| `/collection reset` | `collection` (name/ID) | See requirements and rewards for resetting |
| `/miss` | None | Show achievements you haven't completed yet |
| `/stats` | None | View your full player statistics |

## Quick tips 💡

- 🗓️ **Plan your daily loop** — Start with `/daily` to refresh your state, then use `/claim cards` for your main pulls
- 💪 **Train regularly** — Maxed cards are needed for ascension, so keep training your best cards every 15 minutes
- 👑 **Save for legendary tickets** — `/legroll` gives you a guaranteed 5★, one of the best paths to rare cards!
- 📊 **Check collection progress often** — Use `/collection list` to see what you still need and prioritize your claims
- 🔨 **Master the smith system** — Forge unwanted cards, liquefy duplicates, and draw exactly what you want!
