# Daily Claims 🗓️

Your daily claim loop is the lifeblood of your collection! This page covers `/daily`, `/claim cards`, and `/level claim` — the commands you'll use every day to grow your card pool and fuel your clouts.

## `/daily` — Start your day

Every day starts with `/daily`. This command resets your claim cost and gives you fresh daily quests.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cooldown | 20 hours (17 hours with **Ruler Jeanne** passive effect) |
| Reward | ❄️ Flakes (scales with daily streak + **Cake Day** bonus) |
| Effect | Resets your claim counter back to 0 |

:::tip
Always start your play session with `/daily`! It resets your claim costs so you're not paying extra for the first card of the day.
:::

## `/claim cards` — Standard card claiming

Use `/claim cards` to pull cards from unlocked collections. This is your main way to grow your collection!

### Optional parameters for `/claim cards`

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `count` | Number of cards to claim at once (e.g., `count:5`) |
| `promo` | Claim using promo currency instead of flakes |
| `any` | Skip autocomplete and claim any random card |
| `boost_id` | Target a specific active event (e.g., `boost_id:christmas25`) |
| `group_type` | Restrict to a group category (e.g., `girlgroups`, `boygroups`) |
| `unlocked` | Only claim from unlocked collections |

### How claim cost works

Claim costs scale up the more cards you claim in a single day. The cost resets when you run `/daily`.

| Claims in a day | Approximate cost per claim |
| --- | --- |
| 1st claim | 50 ❄️ |
| 2nd claim | 100 ❄️ |
| 5th claim | 250 ❄️ |
| 10th claim | 500 ❄️ |
| 30th claim | 1,500 ❄️ |

**Total for 10 claims in a row:** 2,750 ❄️
**Total for 30 claims in a row:** 23,250 ❄️

### Special claim bonuses

- **Tohru effect** — If you have **Gift From Tohru** passive equipped and haven't claimed any cards today, your first claim has increased chance at a 3★ card.
- **Boost drops** — During active event boosts, every 30th claim guarantees a boost card drop.
- **Claim Recall active** — Using `/effect use effect_name:claimrecall` reduces your claim count by 4, significantly lowering costs!

:::tip
Check your balance with `/balance` before claiming to avoid overspending. Claim in smaller batches to keep costs manageable during boost events!
:::

## `/level claim` — Level-based claiming

This is how you get new cards **with levels**! Cards between 1★ and 3★ have a possibility of dropping with up to **40%** of their maximum level. Feed these directly to your clout targets with `/combine` to level them up faster!

:::tip
Use `/level claim` to get more combine fodder and speed up your ascends!
:::

### Optional parameters for `/level claim`

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `count` | Number of cards to claim (max 10 per command) |
| `boost` | Apply an active boost ID for special card drops |
| `group_type` | Filter by group type (e.g., girlgroups, boygroups) |
| `promo` (deprecated) | Use promo currency during special events |
| `any` (deprecated) | Bypass guild lock restrictions |

### Level claim bonuses

- **Pre-leveled cards** — Drawn from your unlocked collections with a chance at bonus levels.
- **Combine faster** — Skip the training step! Pre-leveled cards give more XP when used as combine fodder.
