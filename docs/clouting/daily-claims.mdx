## `/level claim` — Get cards with levels!

This is an alternate way to claim cards! Unlike regular `/claim cards`, these cards can drop with up to **40% of their max level** already filled. Feed 'em to your main cards with `/combine` for quick level-ups! 📈

### How claim cost works 💰

Same as regular claiming — costs go up the more you claim, resets on `/daily`.

| Claims in a day | Cost trend |
| --- | --- |
| First claim | Starts at 50 ❄️ |
| Each extra claim | +50 ❄️ each time |
| 30 claims | **Guaranteed promo drop during event boosts!** 🎉 |

### Optional parameters for `/level claim`

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `count` | How many cards to claim (max 10 per command) |
| `boost` | Use an active boost ID for special event card drops 🎁 |
| `group_type` | Filter by group type (e.g., girlgroups, boygroups) |

:::tip
Check `/balance` before claiming so you don't overspend! Claiming in smaller batches keeps costs low. 💡
:::

### Special claim bonuses ✨

- **Tohru effect 🐉** — If you have **Gift From Tohru** passive and haven't claimed today, your first claim has a higher chance at a 3★!
- **Boost drops 🎁** — During active boosts, every 30th claim guarantees a special card drop.
