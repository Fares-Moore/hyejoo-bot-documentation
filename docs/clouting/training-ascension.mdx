# Training, Combining & Ascension ⬆️

Leveling up your cards and combining them into higher rarities is a key part of progression. This page covers training, ascending, and combining cards.

## `/train` — Level up your cards

:::tip
Train cards regularly, especially ones you plan to ascend. Pair training with `/combine` and `/ascend` to push for a clout!
:::

Use `/train` to give a card experience points. Training is how you max out cards so they're ready for ascension.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cooldown | 15 minutes |
| Card query | Any card from your inventory |
| Effect | Gains XP toward next level |

### Max XP per star level 🏆

Your target card can only hold so much XP before it's maxed out:

| Star level | Max XP |
| --- | --- |
| 1★ | **1,250 XP** |
| 2★ | **2,500 XP** |
| 3★ | **5,000 XP** |

Once a card reaches its max, it's ready for ascension! The bot will warn you if your target is already at max.

---
## `/combine` — Fuse fodder cards

Combine lets you transfer XP from one card (the **fodder**) into another (the **target**), speeding up your training. This is especially useful for cards obtained via `/level claim` that already have levels on them!

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Required | Two cards of any star level between 1★ and 3★ |
| Result | Your target card gains XP; the fodder card is **deleted** |
| Cost | Flakes ❄️ (scales with number of combines you've done today) |

### How card levels affect the result 📊

The amount of XP your target card gains depends on **three factors**:

| Factor | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Target's current XP** | Base XP gain is calculated from the target card's current XP using a formula. Higher-XP targets give better returns. |
| **Fodder's level** | Each level on the fodder card adds a flat bonus of `0.2 × card level` to the XP gained. Higher-level fodder = more XP! |
| **Random multiplier 🎲** | A hidden roll from `×1.0`, `×1.5`, or `×2.0` is applied. About 1 in 8 combines get a bonus multiplier. |

### Combine rules 📏

| Rule | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Star range** | Both cards must be between **1★ and 3★**. 4★ and above cannot be used. |
| **XP required on fodder** | The fodder card **must have XP/levels** — you can't combine a freshly claimed card with no XP. |
| **Duplicate restriction** | You can't combine if either card has multiple copies (`amount > 1`). |
| **Lottery cards** | Lottery cards cannot be used in combines. |
| **Favourite protection** | The last favourited copy of a card is protected from being used as fodder. |
| **Hidden promo levels** | Some 1★ promo cards from special events can drop with hidden levels — these can still be used as combine fodder. |

### Cost calculation 💰

Combine costs scale based on how many times you've already combined **today**:

| Combines today | Cost bracket |
| --- | --- |
| 1st – 5th combine | Cheaper rate (lower per-attempt scaling) |
| 6th+ combine | Higher long-tail rate |

The exact cost also depends on the **fodder card's evaluation** — higher-value fodder costs more to combine.

### Tips 💡

- `/level claim` cards are **perfect combine fodder** — they come with levels pre-loaded, saving you training time!
- Check your cards' current XP progress in `/level list` to see how close it is to maxing out.

## `/ascend` — Combine into higher rarity

Ascension combines **two maxed-out cards** of the same star level into a **single higher-star card**. This is the main way to push your collection toward rarer tiers.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Required | Two cards of the same star level, both at max XP |
| Result | One card of the next star level (e.g., 2★ + 2★ → 3★, 3★ + 3★ → 4★) |
| Cost | Flakes ❄️ (base cost scales with card level) |
| Limitations | 4★ and above cards cannot be ascended, nor can cards from fragments, album, liveauction, jackpot, birthdays, limited, signed, or removed collections |

### Ascension rules

- Both cards must be at the **same star level**.
- Cards must be **fully trained** (max XP for their level).
- You cannot ascend the **last copy** of a favorited card.
- The resulting card comes from a pool that excludes certain collection types.

:::warning
Ascension costs flakes ❄️ based on the cards' evaluations. Higher level ascensions cost more, but the **Cherry Blossoms** passive effect reduces forge/ascend costs by 50%!
:::

## `/forge` — The Smith System

Forging is a more advanced card combination system that uses **two cards of the same star level** to create a new card while also earning **vials 🧪**.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Required | Two cards of the same star level (can't be the same card with multiple copies) |
| Cost | Flakes ❄️ (calculated from card evaluations) |
| Reward | A new card of the same star level + vials |
| 4★ forging | Costs 7x more but produces no vials |
| Group tag | The result's group tag (boygroups, girlgroups, etc.) follows the input cards' tags. |
---
## Forging rules

### General rules 📏

| Rule | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Eligible cards** | Must be from non-excluded collections (no fragments, limited, birthdays, etc.) |
| **Specialty collections** | Cannot forge from limited, birthdays, liveauctions, or fragments |
| **Cost reduction** | **Cherry Blossoms** passive effect reduces forge costs by **50%** |
---
### Collection matching 🎯

| Input cards | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Two cards from the **same** collection | A card from that same collection (e.g., 3★ -ive + 3★ -ive → 3★ -ive) |
| Two cards from **different** collections | A card from a **completely random** collection |

---
### Promo card forging 🎉

| Input cards | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Two promo cards from the **same** promo collection | A promo card from that collection (e.g., 3★ -anniversary26 + 3★ -anniversary26 → 3★ -anniversary26) |
| Two promo cards from **different** promo collections | ❌ A **normal** card — the result will **not** be a promo card! |

:::warning
Forging promos from different collections wastes the promo value — the result won't be a promo card!
:::
---
### Album card forging 💿

| Input cards | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Two album cards from the **same** album collection | An album card from that collection (e.g., 4★ -bgalbums + 4★ -bgalbums → 4★ -bgalbums) |
| Two album cards from **different** album collections | An album card from **either** collection (e.g., 4★ -bgalbums + 4★ -ggalbums → 4★ -bgalbums or -ggalbums) |
| An album card + a **normal** card | ❌ A **normal** card — not from either album collection! |
---
### Lottery card forging 🎰

Lottery cards can be forged, and have a chance to drop when forging normal cards of the same rarity.

:::tip
Craft the `cherrybloss` effect to halve your forge and ascend costs — it pays for itself quickly if you do a lot of forging!
:::
