# Collection Progress 📋

Tracking, completing, and resetting collections is the core of clouting. This page covers all the commands for managing your collection progress.

## `/collection list` — Your progress at a glance

Shows your collection completion status across all collections or a filtered subset.

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `cols` | Filter by specific collection names or IDs |
| `completed` | Show only completed (`true`) or incomplete (`false`) collections |
| `clouted` | Show only clouted (`true`) or unclouted (`false`) collections |
| `sortComplete` | Sort by completion status |

### Completion logic

- **Normal collections**: All cards in the set must be owned.
- **Fragment collections**: Only 1★ cards need to be owned (higher stars come from fragment merging).

## `/collection info` — Collection details

View detailed info about a specific collection, including all cards in the set and your ownership status.

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `collection` | Name or ID of the collection to inspect |
| fields shown | Card names, star levels, whether you own them |

## `/collection reset` — Reset completed collections

When you've completed a collection, resetting it earns you a **clout**, **XP**, **Flakes ❄️**, and sometimes a **legendary card or ticket**! This section covers the basics — for the full deep-dive (card consumption, fragment collections, what happens without a 4★/5★), head over to the **[Draw & Legendary Roll page](./draw-and-legroll#collection-reset--turn-completed-collections-into-clouts)**! 📖

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `collection` | The completed collection to reset |
| Shows | Requirements, cards to be consumed, and rewards |

### Quick summary

- You must own **every card** in the collection (see [special cases](./draw-and-legroll#reset-requirements-)).
- Resetting **consumes cards** and rewards you with a clout, XP, and flakes.
- If the collection has a **legendary** (5★ for normal, 4★ for fragment), you get a ticket or card too!
- Check the **[full guide here](./draw-and-legroll#collection-reset--turn-completed-collections-into-clouts)** for all the juicy details! 🎯

## Collection types

Understanding different collection types helps you plan your clouting:

| Type | How to complete | Reward on reset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Normal** | Collect all cards in the set | Legendary **ticket** if a 5★ exists |
| **Fragments** 🧩 | Collect all **1★** cards (higher stars from fragment system) | **4★ card** added directly if one exists |
| **Promo** | Limited-time event collections | May or may not have a legendary |
| **Album** | Special album collections | Depends on the set |
| **Lottery** | Rare lottery collections | Depends on the set |
| **Legendary (5★)** | Collect all 5★ cards | Rarest cards in the game |
| **Limited/Birthdays** | Time-limited special cards | May not be re-released |

## The clout system

When you complete a collection:

1. You can **clout** it — marking it as completed and adding to your total clout count.
2. Use `/collection list clouted:true` to show only collections you've clouted.
3. Use `/collection list clouted:false` to see collections still waiting to be completed.
4. Use `/collection list completed:true` to see which sets have all cards collected.

## Clouted collections

The `clouted` filter in `/collection list` shows the status of your clout progress:

- **clouted:true** — Collections you've already completed and clouted
- **clouted:false** — Collections you haven't yet completed (or completed but not yet clouted)

## Quick tips 💡

- **Filter by what's missing** — Use `/collection list completed:false` to see what collections still need work!
- **Focus on one set at a time** — Pick a collection and chip away at it for steady progress.
- **Check `/collection info` often** — It shows you exactly which cards you're still missing.
- **Don't forget to clout!** — After completing a collection, actually clout it to mark your progress! 🏆
